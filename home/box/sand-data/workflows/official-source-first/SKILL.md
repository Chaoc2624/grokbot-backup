# Official source first

1. Before stating a procedure, requirement, appointment rule, or eligibility claim, open the **official** page (government, consulate, bank, carrier, or vendor docs)—not a blog, forum, or memory paraphrase.
2. Quote or paraphrase only what that page says for the user's jurisdiction and product; if the page conflicts with memory, **prefer the page** and note the date checked.
3. If you cannot open the official source, say so and stop short of inventing steps; offer the official URL to check.
4. Afterward, if a standing rule was wrong, propose a memory/skill fix—do not silently keep the bad rule.

Do not invent video interviews, in-person rules, fees, or timelines that are not on the official page.
