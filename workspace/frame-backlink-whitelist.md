## 三、7 个纯免费站点详细提交流程档案

### 1. Brownbook (`brownbook.net`, DR 80, Rank #286)
- **主页**：https://brownbook.net/
- **提交页**：https://www.brownbook.net/add-business
- **分类**：`other` | **费用说明**：Basic business listing is free.
- **简介**：Global business directory with a basic listing form and website field.
- **硬性要求**：Search for an existing listing before adding a duplicate.；Provide the business name, category and country.
- **操作步骤**：Search for the business name and town. -> Open Add a business and enter the required business details. -> Add the business website and complete the remaining submission step.
- **实战提示**：Use consistent business details. The form includes website and social fields; claiming an existing listing gives you control of its information.

### 2. Business Software (`business-software.com`, DR 49, Rank #432)
- **主页**：https://business-software.com/
- **提交页**：https://www.business-software.com/add-your-product/
- **分类**：`saas-directory` | **费用说明**：Free
- **简介**：Business software listing site with an Add Your Product page. Approved products get a public page.
- **硬性要求**：Provide your name, email, company name and product name.；Complete the CAPTCHA and agree to the publishing/marketing disclaimer.
- **操作步骤**：Open Add Your Product. -> Complete the required product and company fields. -> Submit the form and watch for follow-up to complete the listing.
- **实战提示**：The team says it will contact you within two business days to finish the submission. Read the disclaimer covering publication and marketing use.

### 3. StartupTracker (`startuptracker.io`, DR 43, Rank #437)
- **主页**：https://startuptracker.io/
- **提交页**：https://startuptracker.io/crowdsourcing/
- **分类**：`launch-platform` | **费用说明**：Official crowdsourcing page states that startup listings are 100% free.
- **简介**：Crowdsourced startup tracker. Added projects get a public page with a link to the site.
- **操作步骤**：Open the crowdsourcing page to verify an existing profile or begin a new one. -> Enter the startup name without Inc/Ltd and the website URL. -> Review any further requirements before submitting.
- **实战提示**：The crowdsourcing page now exposes startup-name and website-URL fields. Check for an existing profile before adding one; the later submission steps were not tested.

### 4. nologin.tools (`nologin.tools`, DR 41, Rank #523)
- **主页**：https://nologin.tools/
- **提交页**：https://nologin.tools/submit
- **分类**：`other` | **费用说明**：Basic listing is free. Adding the site badge gives priority review and a ranking boost.
- **简介**：Directory of tools whose core features work without an account. Basic listings are free and reviewed.
- **硬性要求**：The tool's core functionality must work without signing up or logging in.；Provide the tool URL and confirm it meets the rules.
- **操作步骤**：Open the submission form and add the tool URL. -> Confirm the no-login requirement and submit for review.
- **实战提示**：One submitter reported submitting on July 30 and seeing the listing live about 20 days later. They checked the listing URL directly because it was absent from the sitemap. Treat that as one experience, not the current review schedule. The official form now says the badge is optional and grants priority review.

### 5. Fundl (`fundl.us`, DR 30, Rank #535)
- **主页**：https://fundl.us/
- **提交页**：https://fundl.us/create-project
- **分类**：`launch-platform` | **费用说明**：A basic shareable traction page is advertised as free; crowdfunding terms are separate.
- **简介**：Project platform with free public traction pages and optional reward-based crowdfunding.
- **硬性要求**：Sign in to create a project.；Provide a project name, description, image and link.
- **操作步骤**：Open Create Project and sign in. -> Add project details and connect relevant traction sources. -> Review the public page before sharing it.
- **实战提示**：The current product focuses on project details and connected traction data. A free page does not prove that every submission is accepted without review.

### 6. Human or Not (`humanornot.co`, DR 14, Rank #304)

### 7. DevPages (`devpages.io`, DR 10, Rank #460)
