### 5. 250 词深度详述 (Full Product Description, ~250 words)
> **Frame Extractor** (`https://frame-extractor.app`) is a fast, 100% client-side web utility built for video editors, YouTube creators, UI/UX designers, QA testers, and machine-learning engineers who need pixel-accurate still images from video files without cloud upload delays or privacy risks.
>
> Traditional online video-to-image converters force users to upload heavy footage to remote servers, imposing strict 100 MB–500 MB file size caps, slow upload queues, re-compression artifacts, and data retention concerns. Conversely, desktop command-line tools like FFmpeg require terminal setup and lack visual frame scrubbing. **Frame Extractor** bridges this gap by decoding video streams directly in your browser's local memory using GPU-accelerated HTML5 `<video>` and `<canvas>` APIs.
>
> **How It Works & Key Capabilities:**
> - **100% Local & Private (Zero Upload):** Your video files never leave your device. Even confidential client footage, unreleased product demos, or private recordings remain completely offline after the page loads.
> - **Massive File & 4K UHD Support:** Instantly open MP4 (H.264/HEVC/AV1), QuickTime MOV, WebM, and AVI files up to **2 GB** and preserve native pixel dimensions up to **4K UHD (`3840×2160`)**.
> - **Precision Single-Frame & Automated Batch Sampling:** Scrub to an exact millisecond timestamp for a single hero thumbnail, or batch extract frames every N seconds (including sub-second intervals like `0.2s` or `0.5s` for YOLO/LoRA AI training datasets), every Nth frame, or by fixed total frame count.
> - **Lossless & Batch ZIP Export:** Download individual stills or package hundreds of sequential frames into a single timestamped ZIP archive in lossless **PNG**, high-compatibility **JPEG** (with adjustable quality), or modern **WebP**.
> - **Zero Friction:** Completely free, zero watermarks, no daily limits, and **no account or login required**. *(271 words)*

## 四、核心特性要点 (Key Features Bullet List)

- **100% Browser-Based Local Decoding:** Uses hardware-accelerated HTML5 `<video>` and `<canvas>` APIs; zero server upload and instant startup.
- **Up to 2 GB Video Files:** Handles large MP4, MOV, WebM, and AVI recordings without cloud upload caps or network timeouts.
- **Native 4K UHD Resolution:** Exports 1:1 pixel-accurate stills up to `3840×2160` without server-side downscaling.
- **Single Frame & Every-N-Second Batch Mode:** Step frame-by-frame or automatically sample frames every N seconds, every Nth frame, or by fixed count.
- **One-Click ZIP Archive Export:** Batch download hundreds of extracted frames in PNG, JPG, or WebP format with timestamped filenames.
- **No Watermark & No Login Required:** 100% free core functionality with zero registration wall—ideal for `nologin.tools` and developer directories.
