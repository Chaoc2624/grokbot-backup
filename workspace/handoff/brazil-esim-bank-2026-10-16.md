# Brazil remote eSIM + bank (researched 2026-10-16 14:15 Asia/Shanghai)
User has CPF (Guangzhou consulate). Wants remote eSIM, cheap keep-alive, then bank.

## eSIM
- Travel data-only (Airalo etc.): remote, no CPF, no +55 number, useless for bank OTP.
- Vivo Tourist Plan: eSIM by email, ~R$55 / 25GB / 30 days, Brazilian number + calls/SMS, passport + international card. Activate within 15 days of purchase; recommend activate after arrival in BR. Source: vivo.com.br tourist-plan.
- Claro Flex Pass: app "international visitor", passport + temporary BR address + card with 3DS. Plans ~15-30 days. Activate on Claro network (arrival). Source: claro.com.br flex-pass-english.
- Local prepaid with CPF: registration usually wants CPF; remote long-term keep-alive from China poorly documented. X reports Tim no longer easy without CPF; Claro online eSIM for locals exists but spam/pain. Steve Lacey (@stevelacey) Sep 2026: CPF+bank+SIM in Brazil is bureaucratic for foreigners.
- Keep-alive: tourist packs are short; not cheap long-term number hold.

## Bank (with CPF, outside Brazil)
- Non-resident CNR: Banco Rendimento (100% online ~2 business days), BTG Pactual CNR. Need CPF, passport, foreign tax ID/NIF, recent foreign address proof, income proof.
- Resident-style apps (Nubank / Inter / C6): often want Brazilian address; better if resident/CRNM. Risk of rejection as non-resident tourist.
